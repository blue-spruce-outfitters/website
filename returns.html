Returns & Refunds

Last updated November 20, 2026

Orders placed through our TikTok Shop are covered by TikTok Shop’s Return and Refund Policy. This page summarizes how it works with us. If anything here differs from TikTok Shop’s current policy, TikTok Shop’s policy applies.

Return window

You can request a return for most items within 30 days of the delivery date.

How to start a return

  • Open the TikTok app and go to your TikTok Shop orders.
  • Select the order and choose Return/Refund.
  • Pick a reason and add photos if the item is damaged, defective or not as described.
  • Once the return is approved, follow the shipping instructions and use the return label provided in the app.

Please don’t mail returns to us directly. Returns sent without an approved return request and label can’t be processed.

Condition

Change-of-mind returns should be unused, in their original condition and packaging, with all parts and accessories included.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, defective or isn’t what you ordered, start a return in the app with photos. We’ll make it right with a refund or replacement.

Return shipping costs

Who pays for return shipping depends on the reason for the return, following TikTok Shop’s rules. Returns for damaged, defective or incorrect items don’t cost you anything.

Refunds

Refunds are issued through TikTok Shop to your original payment method once the return is received or approved. Your bank or card issuer may take a few extra days to post it.

Items that can’t be returned

Some items aren’t eligible for change-of-mind returns under TikTok Shop’s policy, such as opened personal-care or hygiene items and final-sale items. These will be marked on the listing where they apply.

Questions

Message us through your order in the TikTok app, or email cody@shopbluespruce.com.
