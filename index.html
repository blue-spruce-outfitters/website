Our story

Named for a tough tree

Blue Spruce Outfitters is named for the blue spruce, a tough evergreen built for high-country weather. We’re a small, independent company that finds useful, fairly priced gear and gets it to you fast.

Everything we carry is chosen for one thing: being ready when you need it, on a trail, in a truck or at home.
