Shipping Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

All Blue Spruce Outfitters orders are currently placed and paid for through our TikTok Shop. This page explains how those orders ship.

Where we ship

We ship to addresses in the United States only.

Processing time

Orders are packed and handed to the carrier within 1–2 business days. Business days are Monday through Friday, not counting U.S. federal holidays, so an order placed late Friday usually ships early the next week.

Delivery time

Our products ship from U.S. warehouses run by our fulfillment partners. Most orders arrive within 3–7 business days after they ship. Carrier delays, weather and holiday volume can add time.

Shipping cost

Shipping charges, and any free-shipping offers, are shown at checkout in TikTok Shop before you pay.

Tracking

Once your order ships, tracking appears in the TikTok app under your TikTok Shop orders.

Multiple packages

If you order more than one item, they may ship from different warehouses and arrive in separate packages on different days.

Address problems

Please double-check your shipping address before you check out. We can’t change an address once an order has shipped. Packages returned to us because of an incorrect address may be reshipped at your cost or refunded minus shipping.

Lost, late or damaged packages

If tracking hasn’t moved in several days, your package shows delivered but you don’t have it, or it arrives damaged, message us through your order in the TikTok app or email cody@shopbluespruce.com. For damage, please include photos of the item and the box.
