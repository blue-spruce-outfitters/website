Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Blue Spruce Outfitters LLC (“we,” “us”) collects through this website and our shop, and how we use it.

Information you give us

When you use our contact form or email us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it to answer you and to follow up on creator, supplier or brand inquiries.

Orders placed on TikTok Shop

Purchases happen on TikTok Shop. Payment is handled by TikTok Shop and we never see your full card details. To fill your order, TikTok Shop shares the information we need with us, such as your name, shipping address and what you ordered. TikTok’s own privacy policy covers the information TikTok collects.

Who we share information with

We share order information only with the companies that help us deliver it: our fulfillment and warehouse partners and the shipping carriers. We do not sell your personal information, and we don’t share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Website data and cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. Like most websites, our hosting provider and the services that load our fonts may automatically log basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and order records only as long as we need them to support you, run the business and meet tax and legal requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing cody@shopbluespruce.com. We’ll respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Our website and shop are not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we update this policy, we’ll change the date at the top of this page.
